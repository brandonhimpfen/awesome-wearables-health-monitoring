## Wearable Devices

Consumer and clinical-grade devices designed to monitor physiological and activity data.

- [Apple Watch](https://www.apple.com/apple-watch/) — Smartwatch with health tracking features including heart rate, ECG, and activity monitoring.
- [Fitbit](https://www.fitbit.com/) — Wearable devices focused on fitness, sleep, and health tracking.
- [Garmin Wearables](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/c/wearables-smartwatches/) — GPS-enabled wearables for fitness and performance tracking.
- [WHOOP](https://www.whoop.com/) — Wearable system focused on recovery, strain, and sleep analytics.

## Health Data Platforms & APIs

Platforms and developer tools for collecting, integrating, and analyzing wearable health data.

- [Apple HealthKit](https://developer.apple.com/healthkit/) — Framework for integrating health and fitness data on iOS devices.
- [Google Fit](https://developers.google.com/fit) — Platform for collecting and analyzing fitness and health data.
- [Samsung Health](https://developer.samsung.com/health) — Platform and APIs for integrating wearable health data.
- [Human API](https://humanapi.co/) — Unified API for accessing health data from multiple sources.

## Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM)

Systems used in clinical and home settings for continuous monitoring of patient health data.

- [BioIntelliSense](https://biointellisense.com/) — Wearable biosensors for continuous health monitoring.
- [Current Health](https://www.currenthealth.com/) — Remote patient monitoring platform for healthcare providers.
- [Philips Remote Patient Monitoring](https://www.philips.com/healthcare/solutions/remote-patient-monitoring) — Solutions for monitoring patients outside clinical settings.

## Fitness & Activity Tracking

Platforms and tools for tracking exercise, performance, and physical activity.

- [Strava](https://www.strava.com/) — Activity tracking platform for running, cycling, and fitness.
- [MyFitnessPal](https://www.myfitnesspal.com/) — Nutrition and fitness tracking platform.
- [Nike Training Club](https://www.nike.com/ntc-app) — Fitness app with guided workouts and training plans.

## Sleep & Recovery Monitoring

Tools and devices focused on sleep quality, recovery, and overall wellness.

- [Oura Ring](https://ouraring.com/) — Wearable ring for tracking sleep, recovery, and readiness.
- [Eight Sleep](https://www.eightsleep.com/) — Smart mattress system for sleep tracking and optimization.
- [SleepScore](https://www.sleepscore.com/) — Sleep tracking and analysis platform.

## Open Source & Research

Open datasets, tools, and research initiatives related to wearable health technologies.

- [Open mHealth](https://www.openmhealth.org/) — Open-source framework for integrating digital health data.
- [PhysioNet](https://physionet.org/) — Repository of physiological data and open research tools.
- [mHealth Toolbox](https://mhealth.jmir.org/) — Research-focused tools and resources for mobile health applications.
